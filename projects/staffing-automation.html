The problem

The staffing operation ran on Excel and email. Every placement — from the first client lead to the employee starting work — was assembled by hand across several roles. The work was slow, repetitive and error-prone: manual data entry invited typos, duplicates and bad data, and the same research and drafting was redone from scratch every time.

Mapped out, the weekly manual effort came to roughly 115 hours a week across the BDR, recruiter and customer-success roles.

What I built

A bidirectional Zoho CRM ↔ Zoho Recruit integration built on a seat-grain data model, where one seat = one hire = one unit of revenue.

Zoho apps

  • CRM — Deals as "Job Requests," a custom Seats module, Accounts, Contacts. The reporting source of truth.
  • Recruit — Job Openings, Candidates, Applications, Offers; a Career Portal for inbound applications.
  • Analytics — seat-grain reporting (revenue, margin, fill rate, time-to-fill, fall-through).
  • Writer + WorkDrive — SLA document generation and per-client filing.
  • Forms + Flow + Creator — the employee onboarding subsystem and Employee Portal.
  • Sign — e-signature on offers.

Deluge logic

  • Outbound Job Request → Recruit Job Opening sync with an idempotent, additive seat fan-out (N positions → N seats; re-sending amends rather than duplicates).
  • Inbound candidate binding triggered on the Recruit Offer made status — the definitive commitment signal — which claims a seat through a CRM Blueprint.
  • Offer-accepted advancement, and fall-through / reopen handling (declined, withdrawn, no-show → seat reopens, reason recorded).
  • SLA generation — a Writer template merge → PDF → filed to a per-client WorkDrive folder, driven from the Seat, Job Request and Client data.
  • A nightly reconciliation sweep — a detect-and-report safety net for stuck seats and orphaned approvals.

Integrations & APIs

  • Bidirectional CRM ↔ Recruit via Deluge invokeurl and COQL, over dedicated connections, with a two-way ID handshake so each system can find the other's record.
  • AI meeting notetakers wired to the Zoho Recruit API to auto-fill interview highlights.
  • Auto-estimated salary margins from hiring history plus monthly AI-assisted research, replacing per-request manual research.
  • A form-driven onboarding chain: Recruit Joined status → pre-filled Zoho Form → Zoho Flow → Zoho Creator employee record → Employee Portal user provisioned.
  • Self-service attendance: employees clock in/out in the Employee Portal, replacing manual tracking.

Architecture

CRM (Job Requests + Seats) ⇄ Recruit (Job Openings + Applications + Offers)
  ↳ Analytics reads the seats · Writer/WorkDrive produce SLAs
  ↳ Forms → Flow → Creator provision the Employee Portal

The result

  • Manual effort cut from ~115 to ~21 hrs/week — roughly 94 hrs/week saved, about an 82% reduction (estimate).
  • The heaviest wins were the slow, repeated tasks — LOI drafting, salary research, SLA drafting and attendance — together ~73 hrs/week, now near-zero.
  • Errors reduced: lead entry moved from free-typed Excel to validated, de-duplicated CRM records; LOIs, offers and SLAs are generated from source data instead of re-keyed.
  • Interview time is unchanged and deliberately left in the "after" total — the system captures the notes, it doesn't shorten the conversation. The savings figure stays honest.
  • The automation spine is built and tested live; the reconciliation sweep runs; the Analytics model is built; onboarding is designed and partially built.
